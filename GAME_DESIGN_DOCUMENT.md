# Vecna's Curse — Game Design Document

## 1. Overview
**Genre:** Asymmetrical PvP horror (1v4).

**Platform:** PC & Mobile.

**Audience:** Competitive players who enjoy high-stakes teamwork and mind games.

**Theme & Inspiration:** *Stranger Things* / *Dead by Daylight* style dread, resource scarcity, and map control.

**Core Fantasy:** One player embodies Vecna—an overwhelming supernatural threat—while four survivors scramble to manage fear, gear up, and defeat Vecna by entering the Upside Down.

---

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Fear Drives Gameplay** — Fear is the primary progression and threat amplifier.
2. **Asymmetrical Tension** — Vecna controls tempo; survivors control coordination.
3. **Risk vs Reward** — Fire weapons are strong but risky to obtain and use.
4. **Counterplay** — The powered survivor counters Vecna’s curse without invalidating him.

---

## 3. Match Structure
**Match Length:** 10 minutes.

### Phase 1: Scavenge (0–3 min)
- Survivors search for items and cassette tapes.
- Vecna scouts and applies pressure to build fear.

### Phase 2: Fear Escalation (3–7 min)
- Vecna actively attempts curses.
- Survivors manage fear and attempt rescues.

### Phase 3: Final Push (7–10 min)
- Survivors enter the Upside Down to confront Vecna.
- Vecna becomes more aggressive and opportunistic.

---

## 4. Win Conditions
**Survivors Win:**
- Defeat Vecna in the Upside Down **OR**
- Time expires with 2+ survivors alive.

**Vecna Wins:**
- Successfully curses (kills) 3 survivors **OR**
- Time expires with Vecna alive and only 1 survivor remaining.

---

## 5. Player Roles
### Vecna (1 Player)
**Role:** Hunter and controller. Uses fear, curses, and portals to isolate survivors.

**Health:** High.

**Strength:** High.

**Mobility:** Medium (teleportation via portals).

**Skill Focus:** Map control, curse timing, surveillance.

### Survivors (4 Players)
**Role:** Team-based objective play, survival, and counterplay.

**Health:** Medium.

**Strength:** Low.

**Mobility:** Medium.

**Skill Focus:** Coordination, resource management, fear control.

### Powered Survivor (1 of 4)
**Role:** The counterplay axis.

**Abilities:** Telekinesis + Telekinetic Throw.

**Unique Interaction:** Can enter the Upside Down to interrupt or reverse Vecna’s curse.

---

## 6. Core Mechanics
### 6.1 Fear System
Fear is a shared threat mechanic that rises and falls based on events.

**Fear Increases:**
- Vecna near a survivor.
- Vecna attacks or curses.
- Survivor alone or in dark zones.

**Fear Decreases:**
- Listening to assigned cassette tape.
- Standing near light sources.
- Staying in a group.

**Thresholds:**
- **50% Fear:** Vecna can start Curse Pull.
- **85% Fear:** Curse success chance increases.

---

### 6.2 Curse Pull (Vecna’s Core Kill Tool)
- Vecna can only begin a curse on survivors with **Fear ≥ 50%**.
- Casting takes 2–3 seconds and can be interrupted.
- On success, the survivor is pulled into the Upside Down.

**Escape Chance:**
- If Fear is **< 85%**, the survivor can escape with a quick-time event.
- If Fear is **≥ 85%**, chance is very low unless rescued.

---

### 6.3 Upside Down (Secondary Arena)
- A mirrored, more hostile version of the main map.
- Cursed survivors appear here.
- The powered survivor can enter to free them.
- The final fight against Vecna occurs here.

---

## 7. Vecna Abilities
1. **Telekinesis (Utility)
   - Pulls survivors slightly or throws objects.
   - Low damage, high control.
   - Short cooldown.

2. **Curse Pull (Kill Setup)
   - Requires Fear ≥ 50%.
   - Interruptible cast.
   - Sends survivor into the Upside Down.

3. **Portals (Map Control)
   - Place up to 4 portals.
   - Used to move between real world and Upside Down.

4. **Mind Rift / Spy (Tracking)
   - Reveals survivors within a radius for a short time.
   - Small fear increase to those revealed.

---

## 8. Survivor Gear & Items
### Weapons
- **Fire Sources (High Effectiveness)**
  - Lighters, blowtorches.
  - Strong vs Vecna but limited fuel.

- **Guns (Low Damage / Knockback)**
  - Used to delay Vecna, not kill.
  - Ammo scarcity.

- **Axes (Medium Damage / Risky)**
  - Melee, strong but dangerous.

### Utility
- **Cassette Tape** (per player): reduces fear when used.
- **Flashlight:** temporarily stuns Vecna and lowers fear.
- **Medkit:** restores health.

---

## 9. Map Design
**Primary Map:** Vecna’s House.

### Zones
- Main House (living room, attic, basement).
- Garden / Graveyard.
- Barn / Shed (weapon stash).
- Secret tunnels for escape routes.

### Upside Down Variant
- Same layout, darker, with environmental hazards.

---

## 10. Combat & Balance
### Damage Types
- **Fire Damage:** High.
- **Melee Damage:** Medium.
- **Ballistic Damage:** Low.

### Balance Goals
- Vecna feels oppressive but not unbeatable.
- Survivors must coordinate to survive.
- The powered survivor is strong but not dominant.

---

## 11. Monetization (Cosmetics Only)
- Weapon skins (axe/flashlight/lighter variants).
- Fear aura particle effects.
- Cassette tape skins.
- Vecna kill animation variations.

---

## 12. MVP Scope (Prototype)
1. Single map (Vecna’s House).
2. Fear system + cassette mechanic.
3. Vecna curse + Upside Down teleport.
4. 2 weapons (axe + lighter).
5. Powered survivor rescue mechanic.

---
